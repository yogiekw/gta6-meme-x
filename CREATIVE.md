# Creative decision — gta6-meme-x-01 (decided in the build session, 2026-11-21)

## The moment
GTA 6 launch week itself: after GTA V (2013), the world waited 13 years for
Grand Theft Auto VI. The meme is about that wait — the most specific,
instantly gettable GTA 6 joke there is (the bounty lists it first: "the
thirteen-year wait since GTA V"). The scene is Vice City / Leonida: a neon
Ocean Drive-style boulevard at dusk, a pink convertible, a laughing outlaw
couple (Jason-and-Lucia energy, original art — not Rockstar's art, not a
re-upload of anyone's clip).

## Why it holds a viewer
The hook is the top caption, readable in one second: "WAITED 13 YEARS FOR
GTA 6". Every GTA fan lived that wait. The picture delivers the payoff
immediately: the couple finally driving through Vice City, laughing — the
fantasy the wait was for.

## The joke and the pitch
The punchline flips the wait into make.fun's pitch: "MY AI AGENT MADE MONEY
ON MAKE.FUN". While everyone waited, the agent was earning. That is the
meme's own voice selling make.fun (criterion 4), not a bare link: the agent
literally did the thing the site promises — people and their AI agents earn
real money there.

## make.fun in the picture (criterion 1)
The official make.fun ghost mark (press kit, mark-1024.png) rides in the
convertible's back seat, large and recognisable even at 480×480, white ghost
with sparkle eyes and magenta star coin — worked into the scene as a
passenger, not a corner sticker. The bottom caption also names MAKE.FUN.

## The post text (X)
"Waited 13 years for GTA 6. My AI agent spent the whole wait earning money
on make.fun — it basically paid for the game 👻
https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01 makefun-porridge-desert-inkwell
@makefunai"

Short, part of the joke, carries the bounty link, the play code and the tag.
Play code for this bounty (from the make.fun API): makefun-porridge-desert-inkwell.

## How it was made (in this session)
1. Base scene generated in-session with the media pipeline
   (entry/assets/media-generation-gta6-meme-base-0-*.webp).
2. Official ghost downloaded from https://make.fun/press/mark-1024.png.
3. Composed with entry/compose_meme.py (PIL, run in-session): ghost pasted
   into the back seat, captions in DejaVu Sans Bold, white with black stroke.
4. QC: viewed at 1080×1080 and at 480×480 (judge size) — text and ghost
   both readable at 480.
